Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use HealNet, we collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and appointment details when you book a consultation or fill out our contact forms. We may also collect basic health information you choose to share with your provider during a consultation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to schedule and manage appointments, send booking confirmations and reminders, connect you with certified doctors, and improve the quality of our services. We do not sell your personal or health information to third parties.

3. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal and medical information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with the healthcare professionals you book a consultation with, and with service providers who help us operate HealNet, such as payment processors and appointment scheduling systems. We require these parties to keep your information confidential.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information at any time. To make a request, please reach out through our Help Desk and we will respond as soon as possible.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
