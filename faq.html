Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book an online consultation?

Use the "Book an appointment" button on the homepage, fill in your email, contact number, and preferred date, then submit the form. You'll receive a confirmation with the details of your consultation.

Can I get a prescription refilled through HealNet?

Yes. Once you've had a consultation with one of our specialists, you can request prescription refills digitally without visiting a pharmacy in person.

Is my health information kept private?

Yes. We take reasonable measures to protect your personal and medical information. You can read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

What if I need to reschedule my appointment?

You can reschedule or cancel an appointment by reaching out through our Help Desk with your booking details, and our support team will assist you.

Do I need to create an account to book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by providing your email and contact details directly on the booking form — no separate account is required to get started.

How do I get a medical note for work or school?

After a consultation, you can request a medical note through the Medical Notes service, and it will be issued digitally within a few clicks.
